# Provisional Final Project Preview

This one-page preview helps you notice useful project patterns during Class 1. The complete brief and rubric will follow after Class 2.

## Provisional direction

Teams will use the BaanDee Living setting and solve a business problem different from the delayed-order copilot. The default supported stack is local Python, CSV/JSON, pandas, small reusable functions, a prepared or justified API boundary, Gradio, and focused tests. AI may be used where it adds justified value. Another tool may be proposed when the team can explain why it fits better.

No extra marks are planned for complexity, cloud hosting, or adding many frameworks. GCP is not required. A reliable, understandable local demonstration is acceptable.

## Expected evidence of learning

- A clear customer or employee need and a bounded product hypothesis.
- Evidence that the team inspected and handled data quality responsibly.
- A working flow whose important tool boundaries can be explained.
- Tests or checks connected to meaningful product risks.
- Attention to privacy, permissions, human approval, and ethical use.
- A short value argument tied to an operational or customer outcome.
- A record of teamwork, individual reflection, and AI/tool use.

## Keep during both classes

Record one design decision, one rejected alternative, one unresolved risk, and one piece of evidence that changed your team’s thinking. These notes will support the later proposal and reflection.

The final scope, submission format, assessment details, and AI-use requirements remain provisional until the Class 2 package is confirmed.
